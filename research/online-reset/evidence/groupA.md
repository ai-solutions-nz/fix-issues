**How it works**

*Prompts*
- Generated by an LLM with web search, from the site excerpt plus brand and competitors:
  - `packages/lib/src/onboarding/suggest-prompts.ts::suggestPrompts`
  - `onboarding/analyze.ts` (`promptMixGuidance`, `TAG_GUIDANCE`, `normalizePrompts`)
  - `onboarding/llm.ts::resolveResearchProvider`, which picks the anthropic, openai, openrouter or mistral API provider's `runStructuredResearch`.
- Deduplication: `packages/lib/src/bulk-prompts.ts::dedupeKey`. Bulk import and tags are supported.
- There is no embedding clustering.

*Engines.* The provider registry is `packages/lib/src/providers/index.ts::providerMap`, configured by the `SCRAPE_TARGETS` env var, format `model:provider[:version][:online]` (`packages/config/src/scrape-targets.ts::parseScrapeTargets`).

- **Official APIs:**
  - **OpenAI API** (`registry/openai-api.ts::runOpenAI`): AI SDK Responses API. With `:online` it adds `openai.tools.webSearch({searchContextSize})` with `toolChoice:"auto"`; otherwise `toolChoice:"none"`.
  - **Anthropic API** (`registry/anthropic-api.ts::runAnthropic`): `client.messages.create` with tool `web_search_20250305` when `options.webSearch`. Retries once on a web-search error.
  - **Mistral API** (`registry/mistral-api.ts`): no web search.
  - **OpenRouter** (`registry/openrouter.ts`): `:online` model suffix.
- **Paid scraping providers that drive the real consumer UIs** (none of this runs in-house; there is no Playwright scraping in runtime code):
  - **Cloro** (`registry/cloro.ts`, `https://api.cloro.dev/v1/async/task`; tasks CHATGPT, PERPLEXITY, COPILOT, GEMINI, AIMODE, GOOGLE/aioverview; `CLORO_COUNTRY="US"` hard-coded). This is the README's recommended default.
  - **BrightData** datasets (`registry/brightdata.ts`).
  - **Oxylabs** (`registry/oxylabs.ts`).
  - **Olostep** (`registry/olostep.ts`, URL templates for chatgpt.com, gemini, copilot, perplexity, google aimode).
  - **SearchAPI.io** (`registry/searchapi.ts`).
  - **DataForSEO** (`registry/dataforseo.ts`): `chatGptLlmScraperLiveAdvanced` with `force_web_search:true`, `geminiLlmScraperLiveAdvanced`, `googleAiModeLiveAdvanced`, organic SERP with `load_async_ai_overview:true`, and the LLM Responses API (`chatGptLlmResponsesLive`, `perplexityLlmResponsesLive`, `geminiLlmResponsesLive`) when a version is pinned.
- `Provider.access` is `"scraped" | "api"`, and `isGroundedApiTarget` flags the expensive grounded-API combination.

*Grounding and web search:* yes, per target with the `:online` suffix. Scraped surfaces always search.

*Extraction*
- **Text and citations:** deterministic per-provider parsers in `packages/lib/src/text-extraction.ts` (`extractTextFromOpenAI`, `extractTextFromAnthropic`, `extractCitationsFromCloro`, `extractCitationsFromDataforseoScraper`, `extractTextFromBrightdata` and others). Parsing is re-run from the stored `raw_output` on every replay.
- **Mentions:** whole-word regex candidate matching (`packages/lib/src/mentions.ts::wholeWordPattern`, which is CJK-aware), then confirmation by an **LLM judge, "Jev" from TypeSafe** (`packages/lib/src/mention-judge/jev.ts`, `JEV_MODEL="jev-1.13.0"`, endpoint `https://api.typesafe.ai/v1/systemone` or via OpenRouter `typesafe/jev-1.13`).
  - `mention-judge/analyze.ts::createMentionAnalyzer` **throws if neither `TYPESAFE_API_KEY` nor `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` is set**. The judge is a mandatory paid external dependency; only a `stub` mode exists, for tests.
  - The judge returns `mentioned` (probability > `MENTION_THRESHOLD = 0.3`), `sentiment` (positive, neutral, negative or mixed, with probabilities), and an evidence passage chosen from sentence options (`passages.ts`).
- **Position:** `analyze.ts::rank()` orders tracked subjects by first-mention offset. Only tracked brands are ranked.
- **Citation categorisation:** `packages/lib/src/citations/domain-categories.ts`, `domain-lists.ts`, `page-classification.ts::classifyPage` (own, competitor, social, google, institutional, editorial).

*Share of voice*
- `apps/web/src/lib/visibility-stats.ts::computeShareOfVoice` computes brand mentions divided by brand plus competitor mentions.
- Time series and leaderboard: `shareOfVoiceTimeSeriesLVCF` and `shareOfVoiceLeaderboardLVCF`.
- Served by `apps/web/src/server/analytics-core.ts::getBrandShareOfVoice`.
- Visibility is the share of runs that mention the brand. Sentiment scores positive 100, neutral and mixed 50, negative 0.
- Precomputed rollups: `packages/lib/src/rollups/*` and tables `rollup_*`.

*Scheduling*
- **pg-boss** (`apps/worker/src/boss.ts`, `apps/worker/src/index.ts`): `boss.schedule("schedule-maintenance","*/5 * * * *")` plus per-prompt singleton jobs (`packages/lib/src/queues/prompt-jobs.ts`).
- Cadence and replication policy: `packages/lib/src/run-policy/policy.ts` (`resolveRunPlan`, plan-based `intervalHours`, `replication`).
- Backoff: `run-backoff.ts`.

*Location targeting:* **effectively US-only.**
- `DFS_LOCATION_CODE = 2840` and `DFS_LANGUAGE_CODE="en"` are hard-coded in `dataforseo-shared.ts`; `CLORO_COUNTRY="US"`; BrightData uses `gl=us&hl=en`.
- A comment in `dataforseo.ts` says "Do not expose country localization yet".
- There is no per-prompt or per-brand country or city field in the schema.

*Data model*
- Postgres with Drizzle. Schema: `packages/lib/src/db/schema.ts` and `schema-auth.ts`, which is generated by better-auth. Migrations: `packages/lib/src/db/migrations/0000…0032*.sql`.
- Tables: `brands`, `prompts`, `competitors`, `prompt_runs` (append-only, enforced by a trigger, with `raw_output json` and `web_queries`), `run_extractions`, `run_mentions` (with `assessments jsonb`), `citations`, `cited_pages`, `reports`, `brand_opportunities`, `organization_settings`, `usage_events`, `secrets`, `rollup_prompt_runs`, `rollup_competitor_mentions`, `rollup_citation_urls`, `rollup_citation_pages`, `rollup_web_queries`, `rollup_sentiment_quotes`, `rollup_dirty`, `pipeline_state`, `background_migrations`.
- Auth tables: `user`, `session`, `account`, `organization`, `member`, `invitation`, `apikey`, `jwks`, `oauth_*`, `sso_provider`, `subscription`, `rate_limit`.
- Row-level security is enabled on the tables (`.enableRLS()`).

*Multi-tenancy, auth and RBAC*
- **Yes.** `brands.organization_id` is the tenancy scope.
- better-auth with the plugins `organization`, `apiKey`, `sso`, `admin`, `jwt` and the OAuth provider for MCP (`packages/lib/src/auth/server.ts`).
- RBAC is defined in `packages/lib/src/auth/permissions.ts` (`ownerRole`, `adminRole`, `userRole`; statements `brand`, `report`, `apiKey`).
- Auth0 membership sync: `apps/worker/src/jobs/sync-auth0-memberships.ts`.
- Encrypted secrets store: `packages/lib/src/secrets/crypto.ts` and `store.ts`.

*Evidence storage:* the full raw provider response goes into `prompt_runs.raw_output`, which is never updated. All metrics are re-derivable (`apps/worker/src/jobs/reprocess.ts`, `derive-runs.ts`).

*Other modules*
- `packages/site-audit`: AI-readiness site audit with `checks/reach.ts`, `read.ts` and `understand.ts`, and parsers for HTML, markdown, robots and sitemap.
- Opportunities: `apps/web/src/server/opportunities*.ts`.
- Shareable reports: `apps/worker/src/jobs/generate-report.ts`.
- MCP server, REST API and OpenAPI spec (`packages/api-spec`), plus TS and Python SDKs.
- Usage and cost accounting: `packages/lib/src/usage/cost.ts`.

**How it works**

*Prompts ("queries") and discovery*
- Manual queries, measurement plans and portfolios (tables `queries`, `measurement_plans`, `measurement_query_sets`, `measurement_query_templates`, `query_basket_versions`).
- LLM-seeded discovery with **embedding clustering and dedup**:
  - `packages/api-routes/src/discovery/orchestrate.ts`, which takes `deps.seed` and `deps.embed` and is location-aware.
  - `packages/contracts/src/embeddings.ts::clusterByCosine`, `computeDedupSimilarityStats` and `pickClusterRepresentative`.
  - Gemini embeddings: `packages/provider-gemini/src/embeddings.ts`.
- Also `packages/intelligence/src/suggested-queries.ts`, `query-categorize.ts` and `query-shape.ts` (branded vs non-brand classes).

*Engines: all official APIs, plus one consumer-UI automation*
- **OpenAI** (`packages/provider-openai/src/normalize.ts`): `client.responses.create` with `tools:[{type:'web_search', user_location:{city,region,country,timezone}}]` and a forced tool ("search-required-v1").
- **Claude** (`packages/provider-claude/src/normalize.ts`): `web_search_20250305` with `user_location` and `tool_choice` pinned to web_search. Message Batches are supported (`batch.ts`).
- **Gemini** (`packages/provider-gemini/src/normalize.ts`): `tools:[{googleSearch:{}}]` through AI Studio or Vertex. Location is **appended to the prompt text** ("searching from city, region, country") in `buildPrompt`.
- **Perplexity** (`packages/provider-perplexity/src/normalize.ts`): `https://api.perplexity.ai/v1` agent API, with location as `user_location` on the search tool.
- **Meta Muse** (`packages/provider-muse`): `https://api.meta.ai/v1`.
- **OpenRouter** (`provider-openrouter`) and any OpenAI-compatible local model (`provider-local`).
- **Answer services** Exa and Parallel (`packages/provider-answer-services/src/services/{exa,parallel}`).
- **CDP consumer-UI automation** (`packages/provider-cdp/src/targets/chatgpt.ts::chatgptTarget`): drives a user's **logged-in chatgpt.com in Chrome** through `chrome-remote-interface`. It types character by character with random delays and extracts source pills (`connection.ts::CDPConnectionManager`, `screenshot.ts`). **ToS risk; ChatGPT only.**
- There are **no paid SERP or LLM scrape vendors for AI answers**. DataForSEO (`packages/integration-dataforseo`) is used for **SEO Labs data** (keyword volume, difficulty, rankings).

*Grounding:* forced on for OpenAI and Claude, always on for Gemini (googleSearch). Retrieval status and contract are recorded per answer (`contracts/src/answer-evidence.ts::deriveRetrievalStatus`, `resolveLocationTreatment`).

*Extraction*
- **Mentions:** **deterministic alias/regex matching** (`packages/contracts/src/brand-matching.ts::compileBrandAliases`, `matcherMatchesText`, `textContainsBrandAlias`; `answer-prose.ts::answerProseForMentions`).
- **Competitors:** `competitor-matching.ts::extractRecommendedCompetitors`, `computeCompetitorOverlap`.
- **Citations:** from provider grounding metadata (`answer-evidence.ts::deriveCitedDomains`, `citationEligibleSources`). Cited URLs are captured (`packages/canonry/src/cited-url-capture.ts`).
- **Sentiment:** optional and **off by default**. It uses TypeSafe Jev through the FSL package, or OpenRouter (`packages/canonry/src/sentiment-openrouter.ts`, `sentiment-worker.ts`).
- **Ranked position:** I found no ranked list-position metric (NOT VERIFIED as absent).

*Share of voice*
- `packages/intelligence/src/share-of-voice-frame.ts::buildShareOfVoiceFrame` computes project mentions divided by project plus competitor mentions.
- It applies publication floors (`MIN_OBSERVED_COMPETITORS=3`, `MIN_OBSERVED_ANSWERS=3`) and a tracked-vs-observed basis.
- Also `mention-share.ts::buildMentionShare` and `visibility-score.ts`.

*Scheduling:* **node-cron** (`packages/canonry/src/scheduler.ts`) reading the `schedules` table, with per-project spread (`scheduleSpreadMs`), a job runner (`job-runner.ts`, `run-coordinator.ts`) and a provider batch poller.

*Location targeting:* **yes, country, region, city and timezone** (`LocationContext`). It is stored on `projects.locations`/`default_location` and `query_snapshots.location`/`requested_context`, and is passed natively to the OpenAI, Claude and Perplexity search tools. **This is the best in the group for API engines.**

*Data model*

### Overlaps

- Canonry and Elmo overlap most. Canonry is a superset functionally, but is single-tenant SQLite; Elmo is the better SaaS skeleton.
- Limelit is a strict subset of Elmo's tracking scope; its value is the deterministic methodology.

- GeoLook's tracking is superseded by every other candidate; only its workflow and asset ideas are unique.

### Red flags

1. **Canonry `packages/integration-typesafe` is FSL-1.1-ALv2** (source-available, Competing-Use restriction) and is bundled into the MIT-labelled `@canonry/canonry` npm build. Do not copy it or depend on the npm bundle without accounting for it.
2. **Terms-of-service exposure from consumer-UI automation:**
   - OneGlanse (Camoufox anti-detect browser + residential proxies + real accounts).
   - Canonry `provider-cdp` (logged-in ChatGPT automation).
   - GeoLook's extension is lower risk because the user submits.
   - Elmo pushes this risk to paid vendors (Cloro, BrightData, Oxylabs, Olostep, SearchAPI, DataForSEO). That still needs vendor due diligence.
3. **Elmo's mandatory paid judge (TypeSafe Jev):** cost and lock-in until replaced.
4. **Elmo is US-only today:** location codes are hard-coded.
5. **Telemetry on by default** in Elmo (PostHog), Canonry (canonry.ai) and OneGlanse (PostHog).
6. **Bus factor:** Canonry has 4 authors; OneGlanse and GeoLook are solo projects and stalled. Limelit is pre-release open-core and points users to its paid cloud.
