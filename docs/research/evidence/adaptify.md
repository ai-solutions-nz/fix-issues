Access date for every source: 2026-11-07.

**How the research was done:** adaptify.ai, docs.adaptify.ai, web.archive.org and the third-party review sites (for example ryandoser.com) could NOT be fetched from this session. WebFetch failed with DNS or blocked errors. Everything below comes from WebSearch result snippets. Those snippets are summaries the search tool wrote from indexed page text, so quoted phrases are as shown in the snippets and may not match the live page word for word.

- **First-party (FP):** adaptify.ai, adaptify.ai/docs/*, docs.adaptify.ai/*, adaptify.ai/blog/*, adaptify.ai/features/*. Two docs hosts exist: `adaptify.ai/docs/` (appears newer) and `docs.adaptify.ai` (older, video-transcript style).

## Other attributes

- **Target customer:** SEO and marketing agencies, for example "AI-powered SEO platform designed to automate the SEO process for agencies"; the GEO beta was offered to "Adaptify agency users". Single-site plans suggest direct businesses are also served. Sources: https://adaptify.ai/blog/adaptify-launches-ai-brand-visibility-geo-this-is-your-beta-invite ; https://www.theaireport.ai/tooldatabase/adaptify-seo (3P).
- **Location / geo controls:** local keyword research (https://adaptify.ai/features/keyword-strategy). Local Map SEO ranking is in beta, and "Adaptify does not provide any tools to manage your Google My Business Profile" (https://docs.adaptify.ai/local-map-seo-ranking-beta). No docs on country or language targeting were found.
- **Data providers:** no named provider (DataForSEO, Semrush, Ahrefs and so on) was found in any source. FP sources mention Bing data for GEO performance, the native APIs of the AI engines, and Google Search Console (https://adaptify.ai/docs/performance ; https://adaptify.ai/features/ai-visibility). Any DataForSEO link would be inference only.
- **Conversion / CRM / booking / lead features:** no evidence found in any source searched. HubSpot appears only as a CMS publishing target (https://docs.adaptify.ai/other-integrations), not as a CRM sync. G2 reviewers list "Integration Issues" (5) as a con (https://www.g2.com/products/adaptify-seo/features, 3P). Not finding evidence does not prove the features are absent.
- **Reviews:** Capterra shows 61 reviews, 100% positive in its sentiment summary, with "starting price $999/month" and a free trial (https://www.capterra.com/p/10023107/Adaptify/reviews/, 3P). Trustpilot is mostly positive (https://it.trustpilot.com/review/adaptify.ai, 3P).

## Confirmed baseline (from first-party docs)

1. AI visibility (GEO) workflow with clusters, prompts and assistant searches, CSV prompt import, and a GEO Potential score (docs/geo-strategy, docs/clusters).
2. Keyword and cluster research with a "Potential" score, and a content plan with configurable cadence and automated article writing (docs/clusters, docs/automated-content).
3. Content QA with 20+ per-site quality checks, custom checks and auto-rewrite (docs/content-writer).
4. Publishing to WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, Duda, Ghost and HubSpot, plus manual publishing (docs integrations).
5. Site audit with SEO, GEO and combined scores (docs/site-audit).
6. White-label portal, custom domain, PDF reports, and agency or customer approval gates (docs/white-labeling, white-label-settings, automated-content).
7. Proposal Mode with Day 3/7/14 follow-up emails and view tracking (docs/seo-proposals, proposal-mode).
8. GSC connection and a performance report (connecting-google-search-console, docs/performance).
9. PR backlink outreach from a client-branded inbox (docs/backlinks).

## Uncertainties

- The exact engine list for scheduled AI testing comes from the feature page, not the docs. Google AI Overviews and AI Mode tracking are not confirmed. The 14-day retest is a marketing claim.
- Current FP pricing is unknown. All tiers above are from aggregators and conflict with each other.
- Backlink volume and DR guarantees conflict (DR30+ up to 20, DR40+ 1–6, avg DR45+).
- GA4, conversion tracking, CRM and attribution: no evidence either way.
- Rank-tracking specifics (engines, frequency, location granularity) are not documented in the snippets.
- Date of the GEO beta launch post ("Feb 25", year unclear; likely 2025).
- Two docs hosts (adaptify.ai/docs vs docs.adaptify.ai) may describe different product generations.

## Limitations of this research

- adaptify.ai, docs.adaptify.ai, web.archive.org and ryandoser.com all failed to resolve or were refused by WebFetch from this session, so no page was read in full.
- All FP evidence is from WebSearch snippets. Those are summaries the search tool generated, which can paraphrase, merge pages or drop context. Quoted phrases should be re-checked against the live pages before external use.
- Search coverage is US-only. Missing evidence (for example no GA4 doc found) is not proof that a feature is absent.
- Several 3P listings are affiliate or aggregator content of unknown freshness.
